What We Cover

The Scams We See Most

🔒

Payment Redirect

A hijacked or look-alike email swaps the wallet address on a real business payment. The money goes to the scammer, not the vendor.

💻

Advance-Fee Fraud

You're promised returns, access, or a payout — but every step costs money upfront. The platform and the earnings are fabricated.

👥

Fake Job & Task Scams

Remote roles that pay suspiciously well, then ask you to deposit crypto to "unlock" earnings or install a "verification tool."

🔍

Pig Butchering

A long con: a fake relationship, a fake trading platform, escalating deposits. One of the FBI's top crypto fraud categories by loss.

🔔

Recovery Scams

After the first loss, a second scammer offers to "recover" your funds for a fee. We charge flat fees precisely so this can't happen to our clients.

🛡

Sanctions Screening

Every check screens against the OFAC digital-currency list and the Blockchain Fraud registry before you move a dollar.
